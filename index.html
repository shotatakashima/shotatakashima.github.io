Wine Mail Intake

A personal tool. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone else.

Wine Mail Intake helps its owner keep a private record of wine purchases and shipments. It reads the owner's own order-confirmation and shipping emails from a fixed list of wine retailers and shipping services, with read-only Gmail access, and proposes records (wine, vintage, number of bottles, dates). The owner approves or rejects each proposal before anything is recorded in the owner's personal notes.

  • Runs on the owner's own computer (self-hosted, not reachable from the internet).
  • Gmail access is read-only (gmail.readonly): it never sends, deletes, labels or marks mail as read.
  • Used only with the owner's own Google account.
